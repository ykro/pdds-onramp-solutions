En `db/queries/monthly_sales.sql` (`CA-3.1`), la consulta necesita tres columnas con estos nombres exactos: `month`, `order_count` y `revenue_cents`. Completa los huecos.

```sql
SELECT
    TO_CHAR(created_at AT TIME ZONE 'UTC', 'YYYY-MM') AS month,
    ? AS order_count,
    ? AS revenue_cents
FROM orders
WHERE ?
GROUP BY 1
ORDER BY 1;
```
